## Baseline (2026-08-18)

The workspace was empty and was not a Git repository. There was no existing build or test suite to run. Maven 3.9.16 and Docker 28.1.1 were present; Maven ran on JDK 26.0.2. No performance measurements had been made at baseline.

## Measurements

Two separately scoped throughput measurements are published: the embedded collection-source/discard-sink harness and the real Kafka-to-Redis/ClickHouse backlog harness. They are not comparable and neither is a production-capacity claim. The serving-query measurement is a separate warm-cache/container round-trip benchmark and is not end-to-end pipeline latency. Performance numbers must include the command, source revision, hardware, dataset, warm-up or backlog conditions, sample count, and raw output.
